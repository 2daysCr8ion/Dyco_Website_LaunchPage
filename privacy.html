Website privacy notice

Effective November 5, 2026. This notice describes information collected through dycosystems.com. Changes will be posted here with a revised effective date.

Notice at collection

Site delivery and security. Requests to view this website expose your IP address, requested page, browser information and request time to hosting and delivery providers, including GitHub Pages and Cloudflare, so they can deliver pages and protect the site.

Contact and newsletter requests. If you submit the contact form, Dyco receives your email, subject and message to respond to your inquiry or subscription request. FormSubmit processes the submission and may present a spam check. Email providers deliver the message. Please do not include sensitive or confidential information. You can request removal from newsletter communications by emailing info@dycosystems.com.

Customer records and audiences. Dyco uses its custom-built CRM to manage business contacts and inquiries. LinkedIn is the current advertising platform for customer-list sharing. Contact identifiers supplied for audience matching can be used to show ads to matching LinkedIn members. This is separate from the website cookie choice.

Advertising and measurement. If you allow advertising, the LinkedIn Insight Tag can share online identifiers, IP address, page URL, time and browser information with LinkedIn to measure advertising and support targeted advertising across websites. LinkedIn may associate visits with its members and collect activity across sites. This can constitute sharing for cross-context behavioral advertising. Dyco’s tag does not send contact-form fields and is disabled on the contact and privacy pages. LinkedIn privacy policy.

Retention. The browser preference is stored locally until changed or cleared. Dyco has not set a fixed retention schedule for CRM inquiries, newsletter records or LinkedIn audience lists. For inquiries, relevant criteria include whether correspondence is ongoing and whether records are needed for follow-up or legal obligations. For newsletter requests, subscription status and opt-out suppression matter. Hosting, email and advertising providers also control records in their own systems.

Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information

Opening this opt-out link turns off the site’s LinkedIn advertising tag for this browser. No account or email is required. Use Cookie settings below to review your choice. Clearing site data removes the saved preference; advertising remains off unless you allow it again. Revoking permission stops future tag loads but does not erase information already sent to LinkedIn.

Customer-list opt-out. To request exclusion of your contact record from LinkedIn audience sharing, email info@dycosystems.com with the email address to exclude. No account is needed. The browser setting alone does not identify your CRM record.

Browser privacy signals

The website honors Global Privacy Control and a browser’s enabled Do Not Track signal by turning off advertising even if it was previously allowed. The opt-out applies to this browser; enable Global Privacy Control or set your choice on other devices as well.

California privacy requests

Where California privacy law applies, residents can request access to or copies of their information, correction, deletion, an opt-out of sale or sharing, or limits on certain uses of sensitive personal information. You may use an authorized agent and exercise these rights without discrimination. Email info@dycosystems.com with the right you want to exercise and enough detail to locate the relevant record. Dyco may need to verify identity or an agent’s authority for record-specific requests. No identity verification is required for the browser advertising opt-out.
