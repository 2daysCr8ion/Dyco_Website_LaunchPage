ABOUT DYCO SYSTEMS

Closing the industrial
chemical loop.

Dyco Systems is developing on-site technology to turn industrial waste salts into reusable acid and base. We bring electrochemistry and systems engineering together to pursue cost savings and more dependable chemical supply for industrial operators.
